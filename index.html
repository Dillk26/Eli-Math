Eli's Strategy Tip: When working with "100 times" or "1/10 of", ask yourself: "Is the answer bigger or smaller?" In 5th grade, multiplying by 10 moves numbers one place value to the left!

📐 Place Value Helper Reference

Thousands Hundreds Tens Ones . (Dot) Tenths Hundredths Thousandths
1,000 100 10 1 . 0.1 or 1/10 0.01 or 1/100 0.001 or 1/1000

Rule: Moving 1 place to the left multiplies by 10 (×10). Moving 1 place to the right is 1/10 of the value (÷10).
